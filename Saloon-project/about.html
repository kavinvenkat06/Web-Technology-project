From a Dream to a Legacy

In 1998, Meera Iyer started Glow & Co. with a single chair and a big dream – to create a space where science meets art, and every client leaves feeling more confident than they arrived. What began as a small salon in Chennai has grown into a pan-India brand, but our philosophy remains unchanged: treat every client like family, and never compromise on quality.

Today, our team of over 5,000 professionals carries forward that legacy, blending international techniques with personalized care. We don't just offer services; we craft experiences.
